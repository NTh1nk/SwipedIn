## SwipedIn — The dating app for job applications

Find your next role with a swipe. SwipedIn turns job hunting into a fast, fun, and personal experience — and helps you send better applications in minutes.

### Why people love it
- **Swipe to shortlist**: Like your favorite dating apps. Right to save, left to skip.
- **AI-crafted emails**: Generate a polished, personalized application email for any saved job.
- **Curated matches**: See roles tailored to your skills and interests.
- **Apply and track**: Open the application link instantly, archive when you’re done.
- **Privacy‑minded**: Your resume is saved locally in your browser; content is only sent to the AI service when you ask it to generate an email.
- **Works everywhere**: Beautiful web experience; optional desktop app for a focused workflow.

### How it works
1. **Upload your resume** (or paste text)
2. **Swipe through jobs** and save the ones you like
3. **Generate a tailored email** with one click
4. **Apply and track** — all in one place

### Who it’s for
- **Active job seekers** who want a faster, more engaging way to discover roles
- **Career switchers** who need confident, tailored outreach
- **Students & grads** looking to stand out quickly
