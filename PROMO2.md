## SwipedIn — Swipe your way into a job you love

Find roles you actually want — then apply in minutes. SwipedIn makes job hunting as effortless as swiping, with AI-crafted emails that help you stand out.

### Live demo
- Try it now: [swipedin.vercel.app](https://swipedin.vercel.app)
- No sign-up required

### What it does
- **Swipe to shortlist**: Right to save, left to skip — fast, fun, and focused
- **AI-crafted emails**: Generate a tailored subject + body for any saved job
- **Apply instantly**: Open the job’s apply link and keep your pipeline tidy
- **Privacy‑minded**: Resume stays in your browser unless you ask for AI help

### Why it’s different
- **Personal**: Matches feel curated to your skills and interests
- **Time‑saving**: Go from discovery to a ready‑to‑send email in seconds
- **Delightful UI**: Smooth animations and a clean, modern interface

### Quick start
1) Open the live app
2) Upload or paste your resume
3) Swipe through roles you like
4) Generate your email with one click
5) Apply and track

### Tech at a glance (minimal)
- **Frontend**: Next.js (App Router), deployed on Vercel
- **Data**: Supabase for job data access
- **AI**: Email generation via Hack Club AI; optional OpenAI for summaries/embeddings
- **Desktop (optional)**: Packaged with Tauri for a focused, offline‑friendly shell
- **Privacy**: Resume content is stored locally in the browser by default
